## Brand & Style

This design system channels Tokyo streetwear precision intersected with Nordic architectural minimalism. It positions wardrobe curation and algorithmic styling as an elite, editorial experience rather than a transactional utility. The interface acts as a silent gallery frame: muted, pitch-dark backdrops with warm bone accents that elevate photography, punctuated by an electric citron pulse when the AI delivers insight.

### Visual Pillars
- **Dark Editorial Canvas:** Deep obsidian voids and warm ecru typography create an immediate high-fashion editorial tension.
- **Precision Streetwear Utility:** Technical metadata, garment specs, and match metrics mimic luxury technical garment labels and techwear hangtags.
- **Tactile Modernist Depth:** Translucent obsidian glass overlays, diffused backdrop blurs, and soft surface gradations establish a tactile, pocket-sized studio atmosphere.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an abyssal dark spectrum to maximize garment color accuracy and evoke a luxury night-mode atmosphere.

- **Primary (`#D4FF00` - Electric Citron):** High-voltage kinetic accent reserved strictly for AI insights, compatibility scores, active status toggles, and primary CTAs. It must never be diluted or applied to vast background surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#F4F1EA` - Warm Ecru):** Archival paper white used for display headlines, key values, and primary editorial typography, imparting warmth against the deep obsidian canvas.
- **Tertiary (`#64748B` - Slate Gray):** Structural neutral for borders, dividers, secondary metadata, and inactive icons.
- **Neutral (`#0D0E11` - Obsidian Void):** Near-black deep ground tone with subtle warmth, preventing optical glare while providing profound depth.

### Functional Roles
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0D0E11`
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card/Sheet):** `#16181D`
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated Component/Modal):** `#1F222A`
- **Glass Overlay:** `rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.72)` with 20px blur and `1px` inner stroke of `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.08)`.
- **Text Primary:** `#F4F1EA`
- **Text Secondary:** `#94A3B8`
- **Text Accent:** `#D4FF00`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the aerodynamic, modern geometry of **Outfit** for editorial titles with the crisp utilitarian neutrality of **Hanken Grotesk** for descriptions. **JetBrains Mono** introduces a high-spec technical layer inspired by architectural blueprints and designer care labels.

### Rules of Engagement
- **Headline Casing:** Mixed case with strict negative tracking for a punchy, fashion-magazine masthead presence.
- **Technical Badges & Meta:** Always rendered in `JetBrains Mono`, all-caps, with slight letter spacing (`0.04em` to `0.06em`). Used for season tags, color codes, aspect ratios, fit percentages, and timestamps.
- **Editorial Copy:** Set in `Hanken Grotesk` regular or medium. Maintain strict character-per-line measures (max 45 characters on mobile screens) for rapid scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The architecture operates on an 8-point base grid optimized for mobile ergonomics, swipe gestures, and edge-to-edge garment visuals.

### Grid & Form Factors
- **Mobile First (Default Canvas):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) margins and `0.75rem` (`12px`) gutters for dense, magazine-like multi-outfit comparisons.
- **Large Handheld / Foldable:** 6-column fluid layout, expanding margins to `1.5rem` (`24px`) and gutters to `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Vertical Ryhthm:** Component stacks use `space-md` (`16px`) default gaps. High-tension section breaks use `space-xl` (`32px`). Safe-area bottom offsets mandate a minimum `88px` clearance to prevent conflict with the floating action bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent obsidian layers, luminous low-opacity outlines, and tinted electric glow rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Layers
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Plain `#0D0E11` matte canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Container):** Solid `#16181D` surface with a `1px` continuous border in `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Item / Overlay Card):** Frosted glass `rgba(22, 24, 29, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, elevated by a soft ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`.
- **Level 3 (Floating HUD / Navigation Deck):** Dual-layer pill suspended above content: background `rgba(13, 14, 17, 0.8)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`, bounded by a hair-thin gradient border: `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.15)` fading to `rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.25)`.
- **Citron Luminescence (AI Focus):** High-performing matches or active interactive states emit an ambient aura: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(212, 255, 0, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a sleek, organic geometry balancing brutalist grid alignments with ultra-soft, rounded corner forms. 

- **Primary Interactive Elements & Action Bars:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) provides tactile, thumb-friendly ergonomic anchors.
- **Wardrobe & Lookbook Cards:** `rounded-3xl` (`24px`) curvature creates a smooth, pebble-like silhouette for high-resolution apparel crops.
- **Category Chips & Technical Tags:** Smooth pill profiles (`rounded-full`) or softened rectangles (`rounded-xl` / `12px`) for nested secondary tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Electric Citron):** Full pill (`rounded-full`), `#D4FF00` fill, solid black (`#0D0E11`) text set in `Outfit` Semibold. Press state scales smoothly to `0.97` with a brightness shift to `#E2F952`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Glass):** Pill shape with `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.08)` surface, `1px` border of `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.16)`, ecru text. On press: `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.14)`.
- **Icon Actions:** `44x44px` circular glass nodes, housing sharp, minimal `20px` stroke icons.

### Match Score & Status Badges
- **Vibrant Match Pill:** Compact capsule (`height: 26px`, padding `0 10px`), electric citron fill with `#0D0E11` text in `JetBrains Mono` label-md (`e.g., 98% MATCH`). Subtle pulse animation on AI generation.
- **Meta Tag / Hangtag:** Semi-transparent dark pill with `1px` subtle outline, ecru or slate text (`label-sm`), displaying brand, size, or material composition.

### Chips & Wardrobe Filters
- **Category Chips:** `height: 36px`, rounded pill silhouette.
  - *Inactive:* Solid `#16181D` with subtle `rgba(244, 241, 234, 0.06)` outline, slate gray text.
  - *Active:* Inverted ecru background (`#F4F1EA`) with `#0D0E11` bold text, or accented with an electric citron dot indicator.

### Cards & Apparel Tiles
- **Outfit Card (Display):** Aspect ratio `3:4` or `4:5`. `rounded-3xl` frame, `#16181D` background. Garment images sit cleanly with an inner soft vignette. Floating overlay pins the Match Score pill in the top-right corner (`space-sm` offset) and technical tags at the lower-left.
- **Empty State / Dropzone:** Dashed `1.5px` border in `rgba(100, 116, 139, 0.35)` across an obsidian glass fill with a central minimal plus glyph.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search & AI Prompt Bar:** `height: 52px`, pill-shaped, `#16181D` interior with `1px` slate border. Leading technical search icon, trailing citron-tinted voice/sparkle prompt button. Active focus state triggers an electric citron glow border.

### Floating Action Bar (Bottom Navigation HUD)
- **Docked Deck:** Centered floating glass pod positioned `16px` above the home indicator. Background `rgba(13, 14, 17, 0.85)` with `32px` blur and high-precision `1px` perimeter stroke. Contains icon-only navigation with the central "Drip Check / AI Camera" trigger styled as an elevated circular `#D4FF00` action node.
